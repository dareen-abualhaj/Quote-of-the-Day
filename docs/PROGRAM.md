## About NextFlows Academy

[NextFlows Academy](https://nextflows.ai/academy) runs structured, cohort-based programs for developers who want to build real things — not only follow tutorials.

Every program follows the same shape:

1. **Learn the foundations** — live sessions cover the concepts and how the pieces fit in a real system.
2. **Build hands-on** — guided exercises and 1:1 mentor sessions toward your own project.
3. **Ship something real** — a finished project you can demo, document, and add to your portfolio.

## What you'll walk away with

- A real, working MCP server — your own project, your own tools, your own GitHub repository.
- Something you can show in interviews and add to your resume.
- May count toward your graduation Summer Training requirement, based on your university supervisor.

---

## How the program works

- Two live group sessions a week (90 minutes each) — taught online over Zoom.
- Three on-site workshop days across the six weeks — hands-on setup, code review, and Demo Day.
- A weekly 1:1 session with your mentor (30 minutes, scheduled around your week) — to unblock you, review your code, and keep your project moving.
- No GitHub experience needed. We start from zero — creating your first account, your first repository, your first commit.

| Week | Focus | What's included |

## Pick your project (Week 2)

You’ll choose your own idea in Week 2. If you’re not sure where to start, these five are the easiest to finish — no paid API keys, no logins, no complicated setup:

| Starter | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Notes & FAQ Search | Search your own notes, fully offline. *(example stubs included in this repo)* |
| Personal Expense Tracker | Summarize spending from a simple spreadsheet. |
| To-Do List | Add, list, and complete tasks. |
| Weather Briefing | Pull live weather from a free public API. |
| Quote of the Day | Fetch a random quote from a free public API. |

Want a bigger challenge? Repo health checkers, course planners, job application trackers, and sports stats servers are all on the table too — ask a mentor before expanding scope.
